    Rules

  • Movement: The bird is constantly moving forward and is pulled down by gravity, causing it to descend automatically.
  • Control (Flap): You control the bird's vertical movement by tapping the screen (or pressing a key/button). Each tap makes the bird flap its wings and ascend a short distance.
  • Obstacles: The screen is filled with pairs of pipes (one from the top, one from the bottom) with a small, randomly positioned gap between them.
  • Scoring: You earn one point for every set of pipes you successfully pass through the gap.
  • Game Over: The game ends immediately if the bird collides with: * One of the pipes. * The ground.
